Ember Run — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Hi! I'm Pritam Maharjan, a solo indie developer based in Berlin, Germany. I built Ember Run as a small arcade passion project. This page explains — in plain English — what data the game touches, who else might see it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email me at emberrun0@gmail.com.

1. What I Collect

On your device only (localStorage): high score, sound preference, death/ad counters used to trigger rewinds, age declaration, and cached ad files (auto-deleted after 30 minutes or when you go back online). I never see this data — it lives on your phone.

Through Google AdMob (third party): when ads load, AdMob may process your IP address, advertising ID, device type, OS version, and coarse location for ad delivery and fraud prevention. See Google's ad policy.

Through Firebase Analytics (Google): the app uses Firebase Analytics to understand usage patterns. This may collect device identifiers, app interaction events, and crash data. See Google's privacy policy.

Through Firebase Crashlytics (Google): the app collects crash reports including device state, stack traces, and device info (model, OS version, memory) to diagnose and fix bugs. See Firebase privacy documentation.

Through Google Firestore (Google): if you are signed in, the app may store your user profile (user ID, display name, high score, games played) in a cloud database. This data is encrypted in transit and only accessible by you. See Google's privacy policy.

What I do NOT collect: no email addresses, no contact lists, no photos, no precise location.

2. Europe (EEA / UK) — GDPR

Before any personalized ads load on EEA, UK, or Swiss devices, Ember Run shows Google's IAB-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) pop-up. You can choose:

  • Consent — see personalized ads.
  • Manage options / Reject — non-personalized ads only. The game still works exactly the same.

You can revisit your choice anytime from Settings → Privacy Options in-game. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for ads, legitimate interest for on-device gameplay storage.

Your GDPR rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority. Email emberrun0@gmail.com and I'll respond within 30 days.

3. California — CCPA / CPRA

If you live in California, you have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (which under CPRA includes cross-context behavioral advertising).

  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: open the in-game CMP from Settings → Privacy Options and choose "Reject" / "Opt out." This sends an opt-out signal to AdMob.
  • You can also limit ad tracking at the OS level: iOS → Settings → Privacy → Tracking; Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
  • I do not knowingly sell personal information for money. Sharing for advertising is disclosed above.
  • I will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K / Worldwide)

Ember Run is rated for general audiences but is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA).

Age Verification: On first launch, Ember Run asks "Are you 16 years of age or older?" This age gate is required under GDPR Article 8 (Germany's digital consent age is 16). Your answer is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to me or any third party.

  • If you are 16 or older: You will see the standard consent form and may choose personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • If you are under 16: The consent form is skipped. Only non-personalized ads are served. AdMob's child-directed flags are activated so that age-restricted ad content is filtered out.

I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data, email emberrun0@gmail.com and I will delete it immediately.

6. Data Retention & Security
